01 / CUSTOMER JOURNEYS

One business chain.
Every commitment has prerequisites.

Turn verbal handovers into records, stages and evidence. Missing customer information needs clarification. Missing device evidence pauses the design. An unapproved quote remains a draft.

◎

From inquiry to qualified lead

ACQUISITION

Record channel attribution, renovation stage, budget and purpose consent. The host model checks qualification and prepares sourced content drafts and proposed experience plans. Publication, invitation delivery and customer confirmation remain separate states.

◇

From design to accepted project

DELIVERY

People record circuits, neutral wire, load and network evidence. Designs bind exact devices and firmware. The runtime computes integer cents, cost, margin and discount, then advances through procurement, installation, commissioning and acceptance after approval.

↺

From fault to traceable resolution

AFTERCARE

Route offline, scene, network, device, safety and privacy faults. Models prepare response plans; people record service evidence and customer confirmation. Reports retain unresolved risks and follow-up plans.

02 / FOUR CONNECTED LAYERS

Understand the business.
Inspect the mechanism.

Four layers share business names, permissions and evidence rules. Procedures, claimed tasks, browser forms and ledger receipts can be checked against one another.

01

Journeys and store operations

JOURNEY

Owners see goals and decisions. Consultants see leads and experiences. Engineers see surveys, designs and milestones. Aftercare staff see faults, field evidence and follow-up work.

02

Capabilities and records

CAPABILITY

19 record types share 39 operations. CLI, MCP and local HTTP use one gateway that checks ownership, consent, compatibility evidence, approval digests and stage dependencies.

03

Employees and skills

WORKFORCE

20 roles have explicit accountability. 35 skills include complete procedures. Five workflow kinds cover acquisition, delivery, aftercare, diagnosis and preparation work for any installed employee skill.

04

Host model and ledger

EXECUTION

Codex, Qwen Work and WorkBuddy connect through stdio MCP using the current host model. The runtime schedules and validates; SQLite commits records, idempotency receipts and hash chained events.

◇

People and commitments

HUMAN DECISION

Quote approval binds a content digest and expiry. Browser owner access requires confirmation in a real terminal. MCP always acts as an agent and cannot approve quotes or attest field acceptance.

↯

Visible failure handling

RECOVERY

Expired leases can be reclaimed. Failures retain history with bounded retries. Changed record versions, corrupted data or missing prerequisites block submission instead of manufacturing progress.

ACCOUNTABLE WORKFORCE

Every role has
a skill, a boundary and an output contract.

Employee tasks bind existing records and their versions. Claiming a task returns the sealed skill procedure. Content, video, finance and research roles prepare analysis, drafts and decision packages, marking external actions and field evidence as pending.

  • ✓All 35 skills can enter the queue: employee-work binds a role to its accountable skill.
  • ✓Preparation has its own result: a report cannot pass as a published video, payment or installation.
  • ✓CEO allocation creates real tasks: management-loop actions become claimable employee workflows.

03

Connect your host

Setup generates project configuration. The host claims tasks, submits the required schema and reads goal reports. Approvals and field evidence produce visible pending work.

Model execution needs an active host session. Background tick schedules work; it does not continue inference after the host closes.
# Use a Node.js 24+ environment
git clone https://cnb.cool/workloom-ai/workloom-BusinessManagementSystem.git
cd workloom-BusinessManagementSystem
node scripts/business-management.mjs doctor
node scripts/business-management.mjs init --demo
node scripts/business-management.mjs setup --client all
node scripts/business-management.mjs serve
# Open http://127.0.0.1:9393 in your browser

04 / EVIDENCE & AUTHORITY

Inspect local results.
Keep external actions explicit.

People retain responsibility for quote commitments, customer consent and field delivery. Compatibility depends on the exact model, firmware, gateway and ecosystem conditions. A protocol label cannot prove it.

● Current connector state and receipt meaning

Publishing, messaging, payment and real device control connectors are disabled. A local receipt proves a SQLite commit; externalVerified=false. Live identifies a real data workspace, whose sources and field facts still require human verification.

Separate purpose consentConsultation, appointments, marketing and aftercare retain independent scope; withdrawal pauses affected work.
Version bound compatibilityMissing or expired exact SKU, firmware and pair evidence blocks design advancement.
Approval binds contentMargin and discount limits remain hard blocks even after human approval.
Ordered field stagesAuthorized people record contract, procurement, installation, commissioning and acceptance.
Will it publish videos, contact customers or control devices?

It prepares local content drafts, production plans, appointment plans and response packages. Publishing, messaging, payments and device control are disabled; approval cannot unlock them. Real integrations need separate authorization, credential custody, platform permission and external receipt validation.

Can all 35 skills execute?

Every skill can be claimed through employee-work and submit a structured preparation artifact. Fixed business workflows also apply deterministic record changes and dependency transitions. Video staff deliver filming and editing instructions; finance staff deliver reconciliation plans. Video rendering, payments and real accounting execution are outside the enabled scope.

How do I move from the sandbox to a real store?

Create a separate live data directory and workspace. Register store limits, actual SKU versions and sourced customer or field evidence. Synthetic records cannot be promoted into real facts. The owner authorizes a session in a real terminal and checks approval digests, contracts and field material.

Industry source check dates are kept in the repository. Official documentation supports general mechanisms, not claims that a store has real accounts, permissions, hardware or customer cases.
