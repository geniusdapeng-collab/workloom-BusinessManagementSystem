BEFORE YOU START

A few practical questions.

Can a lead start with only a name or building?

Yes. Keep the source and known information. AI enrichment is a pending proposal; a human confirms facts and consent before applying them.

How do Huawei and Aqara relate to this store?

They provide official knowledge and experience references. Shuguang is fictional and makes no dealership claim. Synthetic prices, SKUs, and compatibility cannot support real sales commitments.

Will AI message customers, sign, or control devices?

External connectors are disabled. AI prepares scripts, drafts, and plans; consent, commitments, and field results require the relevant human confirmation and evidence.

Does the store keep reasoning after the AI host closes?

SQLite records persist. The scheduler does not call a model. New model work needs an active host session, and the next session can continue claiming tasks.

How can a real store start?

Rehearse in sandbox, then create a separate live directory, tenant, and workspace. Live rejects synthetic history and evidence. Use controlled contact references.

Is this a signed and fully accepted desktop product?

The deliverable is public source and a local browser workbench. Signed installers, other client devices, real platforms, hardware, and customer operations need their own acceptance evidence.
